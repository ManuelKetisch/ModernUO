## Finding and digging up a chest

Treasure hunting requires a map, a shovel, Cartography, and Lockpicking. Mining is optional and increases the distance from which a character can dig. Monsters in Trammel and Felucca can drop maps for either facet, with harder monsters dropping higher-level maps.

- **Trammel.** Match the map pin to a location number in the [map archive cited by the wiki](http://uo.stratics.com/thb/info/maparchive/Archive1.shtml), then recall using the matching rune from a rune library. The wiki also [cites an EasyUO forum thread](http://www.easyuo.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=7619) for obtaining the map number.
- **Felucca.** Use the coordinates for the pin and navigate with a sextant. The wiki says recall to the chest spot is unavailable.

Use the shovel on the map or select **Dig for treasure** from the map's context menu. Moving, moving items in the pack, or casting cancels digging. You can defend yourself in place. Taking items from a chest can spawn more guardians, even when you take single items from a stack. Remove the chest through its context menu when finished. Otherwise another hunter at that spot has to wait for it to decay.

## Chest levels and loot

The source table lists Cartography and Lockpicking together. The wiki flags the thresholds as potentially outdated after the May 2017 update. It reports that level 7 requires effective Lockpicking above 120 and that +1 gear suffices at 120 natural skill. It says level 8 needs multiple +3 pieces and gives no exact threshold. Level 7 and 8 guardians have increased difficulty and yield more weapon experience than their usual versions. Level 8 can contain a [random Paragon artifact](https://www.uoex.net/wiki/Paragon_Artifacts). The wiki leaves its other loot counts and guardians unspecified.

### Special loot

Each item above the lower end of a chest's item range indicates one special item, up to four. The four tiers all list a Bucket and edible Coconuts in three graphic variants. Chests can also contain weapons and armor with various attributes.

| Special loot tier | Other possible special loot |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Lantern of Souls, Crystal Ball, Soulstone Shards (type 1), Novice +1 Lockpicking and Cartography gear |
| 2 | Soulstone Shards (type 2), Adept +3 Lockpicking and Cartography gear |
| 3 | Soulstone Shards (type 2), Master +5 Lockpicking and Cartography gear |
| 4 | Soulstone Shards (type 3), Master +5 Lockpicking and Cartography gear |

### Maps and secret stash notes

- Ordinary monster drops reach level 5. A Paragon chest from a monster that drops level 5 maps can contain a level 6 map.
- Opening a treasure chest can yield a map one level higher, up to level 8. The map's facet is random. For level 5 chests at 3,000+ Luck, players reported around 50% in Felucca and around 25% in Trammel.
- The wiki estimates a 10% chance for a secret stash note one to three levels below the chest.

## Lockpicking gear and tenacity

Skill gear can boost Lockpicking and Cartography above their normal caps. Pieces have up to 100% tenacity. Each use reduces tenacity, and at 0% the piece stops granting the bonus. The wiki lists no way to repair tenacity.

The May 2017 update introduced timed lockpicking. Pick material and the difficulty of the map affect the attempt along with the player's skill. The wiki notes that picks may break more often on harder chests.
