---
title: A solo owner can breed two stabled pets
feature: pet-systems
type: decision
status: verified
verified: 2026-11-24
---

Start `[PetBreed` near an animal trainer to select either or both parents from
your stables, including a pair whose combined slots exceed the active follower
limit. Claiming each offspring requires room in the active roster. A dedicated
breeder also supports a confirmed partnership between two owners.
