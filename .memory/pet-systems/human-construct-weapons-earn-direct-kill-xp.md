title: Human construct weapons earn direct kill XP
feature: pet-systems
type: invariant
status: verified
verified: 2026-11-24
---

A human-form Bio Construct can wear compatible jewelry. Its wielded,
activated weapon earns the standard player award for a qualifying direct
kill while its owner is online within 18 tiles on the same map. The owner's
weapon receives no second award for that kill. Other eligible pet kills use
the existing owner weapon rules.
