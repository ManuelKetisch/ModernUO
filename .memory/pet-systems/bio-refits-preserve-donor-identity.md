feature: pet-systems
type: invariant
status: verified

---

A trained owner can use `[BioRefit` at an anvil and forge to change an assembled
construct's frame, combat module, or utility module. The refit preserves body,
trait, stats, skills, gear and bonding. Installed parts and effect cooldowns
are saved. Version zero constructs migrate with an Iron frame and empty slots.
