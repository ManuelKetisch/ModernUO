A qualifying defeat from either shipped Destard Ancient Wyrm spawner grants
a saved credit and scale to one eligible recipient. `[WyrmRewards` claims
pending drops and exchanges six credits for a blueprint. `[HybridForge`
turns ordinary equipment into a polehammer or scale-lined plate at an anvil
and forge after the smith learns the corresponding recipe.
