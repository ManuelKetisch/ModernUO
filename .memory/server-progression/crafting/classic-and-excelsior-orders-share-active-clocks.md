---
title: Classic and Excelsior orders share active clocks
feature: server-progression
type: invariant
status: verified
verified: 2026-11-24
---

Classic remains the default order ruleset. An opted-in character's future
smith and tailor offers use the Excelsior interval and reward rules, while
the existing active clock still gates the next offer. Book entries and
deeds preserve the selected reward rules independently of current settings.
