The Bone Carving group in Carpentry uses the original Vetus Mundus ingredient counts and requires 80 Carpentry and 105 Necromancy. The Bone Scythe uses the normal scythe combat mechanics. The Skull Heap uses the standard house container access rules and stores jinxed, champion, and golden skulls. Bone Knights yield jinxed skulls and wild Hell Hounds yield hellhound ribs as death loot.
