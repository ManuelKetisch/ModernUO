The weapon leveling exchange sits east of Trinsic bank at 1820, 2828 on both
Britannia facets. The activation deed costs 100,000 local recycling tokens.
Hostile kills and ordinary enchanted equipment recycling supply the tokens.
This local exchange also sells the change, re-layer and ten-level boost deeds.
